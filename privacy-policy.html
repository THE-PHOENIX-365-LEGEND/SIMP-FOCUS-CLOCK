Privacy Policy for SIMP-FOCUS-CLOCK

Last updated: September 3, 2026

SIMP-FOCUS-CLOCK is a productivity and time-management application that provides Alarm, Stopwatch, Timer, and Focus features.

Information We Collect

SIMP-FOCUS-CLOCK is designed to operate primarily on your device. The application does not require you to create an account.

The application does not intentionally collect or sell personal information such as your name, address, contacts, photos, or messages.

Accessibility Service

The Focus feature uses Android's Accessibility Service to identify when a selected application is being used and to restrict access to that application during an active Focus or cooldown session.

Accessibility Service data is used only for the Focus functionality of the application. The application does not intentionally transmit the contents of other applications to a remote server.

Notifications

If notification permission is granted, SIMP-FOCUS-CLOCK may display notifications related to Focus sessions, cooldowns, and timers.

Alarms

The Alarm feature uses Android's alarm scheduling system to trigger alarms at times selected by the user.

Data Storage

Information required for application functionality may be stored locally on your Android device. We do not operate a server for collecting this application data.

Third-Party Services

SIMP-FOCUS-CLOCK does not intentionally send user data to third-party analytics or advertising services.

Children's Privacy

SIMP-FOCUS-CLOCK does not intentionally collect personal information from users.

Security

We take reasonable steps to keep application data local and minimize unnecessary collection of information.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application changes. Any updated version will be published on this page.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or SIMP-FOCUS-CLOCK, please contact the developer using the support email provided on the application's store listing.
